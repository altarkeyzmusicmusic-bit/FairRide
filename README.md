# F# FairRide

FairRide is a South African transport platform connecting commuters, drivers and vehicle owners.

## Our Mission

To create a fair transport system that protects commuters, drivers and vehicle owners.

## Platform Applications

1. FairRide Commuter
2. FairRide Driver
3. FairRide Vehicle Owner

## Core Business Rules

- Vehicle owners purchase prepaid trip units.
- Each eligible accepted trip deducts R13 from the owner's prepaid units.
- Passenger fares belong to vehicle owners.
- Cash and electronic payments are supported.
- Drivers are matched using proximity and eligibility.
- Vehicle VINs and number plates must be unique.

## Technology
